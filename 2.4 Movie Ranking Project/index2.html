Favorite Movies According to Sylvita La Del Barrio

My Top 4 Movies Of All Time.

True Romance

Awesome script, directing, editing, writing, acting... and that soundtrack. Truly an epic, unique love story.

Traffic

I worked on this movie, so it's near and dear to my heart. Steven Soderbergh is a fantastic director/storyteller. And this movie has an amazing cast with Jacob Vargas, Benicio del Toro, Catherine Zeta-Jones, Clifton Collins Jr., and more!

Terminator 2

Classic action-adventure sci fi! This movie is a classic and holds up in the test of time! True storytelling and fantastic all around!

Aliens 2

Fantastic movie, great storytelling, great soundtrack, and original awesome actors.
